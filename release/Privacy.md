# Daywell privacy policy
Daywell stores your plan and settings in private app storage. Your plan contains the names, times, colours, icons and notes you enter. You may choose to copy your plan directly to a paired watch over Bluetooth or save a JSON copy through the device's local document picker. Each device keeps its own settings. Daywell does not operate a server, create accounts, include advertising or analytics, or request internet access. It does not enable Android automatic backup. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. Manually exported files and copies transferred to another device must be deleted separately.
